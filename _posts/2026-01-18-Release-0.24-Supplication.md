* Release Notes

On [itch.io](https://haikuinteractive.itch.io/dwerg-saga). Access for [patrons](https://haikuinteractive.itch.io/dwerg-saga/patreon-access).

All the features in this release come from making a scripted story possible. This story is for the proud builders and crafters in your clan.

Features:
- Buildings and Items must be invented before they can be built or crafted. Each has an introductory storylet.
- Discovery direction can be influenced by directing a Dwerg's conversation from the Knowledge tab.
- Building and Item damage and repair.
- Tool condition affects speed of work. Weapon condition affects damage. Armour condition affects protection. Damaged food can give Dwergs a stomach bug.
- Swords can be crafted at Bronze Weapon-smiths.

Chores:
- Added Linux support.
- Update to .NET 8 and MonoGame 3.8.4.
- Transition Mac save files from old pre .NET 8 location.
